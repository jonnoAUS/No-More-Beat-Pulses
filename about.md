# <cy>No More Beat Pulses</c>

<cr>No More Beat Pulses</c> exists for one simple reason: to make some levels feel a little <cg>less distracting</c>.

A lot of objects in Geometry Dash use subtle beat pulsing, which can look nice but in some levels it can also make it harder to <cy>focus on gameplay</c> or read the screen comfortably.

This mod disables those beat pulses on selected objects so the game looks a bit more <cg>stable</c>, <cb>clean</c> and easier to focus on.

## <cy>Why use it?</c>

- <cg>Less visual noise</c>
- <cy>Easier to focus</c> on what matters
- <cb>Cleaner-looking</c> gameplay and decoration
- Great for those who prefer a more <co>static</c> look

<cg>Made for players who just want the level to "chill out a bit."</c>
